Two frames, one stylesheet apart.

The left frame runs the original stylesheet against the current markup and the current engine. The right frame runs the rebuild. Nothing else differs — not a line of JavaScript, not a string, not a row of data. That is the honest way to show a redesign: if the improvement survives being measured against everything except the design, it was the design.

Both frames open on the same seeded log: 112 kg, three days a week, 5.5 hours of sleep, rotating nights.

Both frames navigate together · scroll inside either one

Kept on purpose

  • The palette — ivory, charcoal, one burnt orange
  • Condensed display type, monospace for every unit
  • Near-square corners
  • Every disclosure the engine makes: estimates called estimates

Removed on purpose

  • Borders and shadows on content blocks
  • Pills for everything that could be a rule
  • Equal weight given to labels and to figures
  • Anything orange that was not a signal
