The mark

Four strokes for the work. One slash for the set that closes it.

The tally is the oldest counting gesture there is, and the app is a counting machine: sets, reps, days since, days left. It is drawn on the oldest surface there is. Nothing about it is borrowed from fitness apps — no flame, no bolt, no swoosh, no shield. It is a record someone made with a stick on a wall, and it means exactly what the product does.

The fifth stroke is the important one. It is not decoration: it is the moment the count is closed. That is the whole argument of the training method.

Minimum size 16 px. Below that the strokes fuse and it becomes a smudge — use the icon instead, or drop the mark entirely.

01

Space & proportion

Clear space = 1× the stroke height on all sides (dashed). No other graphic element, type or edge may enter it.

Construction — 24 × 24 grid

margin 3.2 · stroke 2.4 · gap 2.667
verticals y 4.2 → 19.8 · slash 19.4 at −16°
all values even-halves of the 24 grid

Everything is generated from these six numbers, so the 16 px favicon and the 176 px cover are the same drawing.

Ink field, ivory mark, no radius of our own — the operating system masks it. Scaled to 66 % so it survives the maskable safe circle.

The mark is the icon. No letter, no container change, no gradient — one field, one gesture. It stays legible at 28 px, which is where most icons fail.

Intended surface ratio — 72 % paper · 16 % recess · 9 % ink · 2 % signal. The orange is measured in square centimetres, not in taste.

Signal — use it

  • The primary action. One per screen.
  • The current position: active nav edge, selected chip, focus ring.
  • One state that needs attention — never all of them.

Decoration — never

  • Headings, borders, or icons whose only job is to look alive.
  • Backgrounds of cards or sections.
  • Two orange elements competing on one screen.

The signature moment

One solid ink block per screen. It answers the only question the app exists to answer.

The verdict is the product. It has to feel like a judgement handed to you, not a notification. Ink, not colour: colour would make it a warning, and it is not one.

Availability is a ruled scale, not a gauge. Reasons are a list with square markers — the marker changes only when the meaning does. The primary action inverts to ivory, so it is the brightest object on the page.

08

Voice

Plain, specific, unexcited. It states what it knows, names what it does not, and never congratulates. Numbers get units. Estimates are called estimates, every time.

No exclamation marks. No “crush it”. No invented data. If something is unknown, the interface says unknown rather than showing a plausible number.

Copy that sounds like the product

“Do not train today. Your chest has three days left.”

“Estimated recovery, not a measurement. Every factor is listed below.”

“No sleep recorded. The verdict uses training history only.”
